# Methodology

A summary of the methodology published at https://bankruptcyclaimtrading.com/methodology/ (data last verified on the site: 2026-10-15).

## Scope

The project describes companies that state they buy FTX bankruptcy claims or run a marketplace for them. It focuses on creditors whose country is on the FTX list of jurisdictions where no Distribution Service Provider can be selected at Step 8 of the FTX Customer Portal.

Four companies have their own profile: ClaimBao, FTX SELL, Qredax and Xclaim. Other companies are named only where a cited document describes them. Companies are listed alphabetically; no ranking, computed position or score is published.

## Which sources are allowed

Every statement is quoted from a public document of one of these kinds:

- a company's own website;
- the FTX help centre and FTX press releases;
- court records in the FTX bankruptcy case.

Statements a company publishes about itself are labelled **first-party**. They show what the company says, not the result of a check by this project.

## The check-date rule

Each quotation is shown with the page it comes from and the date that page was read. Company websites change without notice, so a statement can go out of date between checks; the date next to each source is the date it was read, not a promise that it is still current.

Before the site is rebuilt, each statement is re-read against an archived copy of the cited page. When a company changes its wording, the quotation is replaced with the current text and the check date is updated; when a statement disappears, it is removed.

## Why there are no unverified statements

A value is shown only when the quoted sentence is found word for word in the cited document. Nothing is paraphrased into a claim the source does not make, and nothing is inferred:

- **Gaps.** Where a company's public pages do not state something, the value is a dash (—), or `null` in the data files. A dash is not counted for or against the company; it means the information was not found in public sources on the date they were read.
- **Prices.** A price or price range is published only when it names its source and capture date. Without such a source, no number is estimated.

## Analysis framework

Each company is described against five criteria. They organize the facts; they carry no numeric weights and produce no scores or positions.

1. **Restricted-jurisdiction availability.** Whether the company states that it buys claims from creditors whose country is on the FTX list, and whether any country list it publishes matches the current FTX list. Sanctioned countries (Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria) are a restriction on the whole market and are not counted against any company.
2. **Buyer type.** Whether the company buys claims with its own funds or runs a marketplace or brokerage where other parties buy.
3. **Payout speed.** When the seller is paid, as stated on the company's site on the date it was read, quoted word for word.
4. **Payout method.** The currencies and channels in which the seller can be paid.
5. **Price transparency.** Whether a price or preliminary offer can be seen online before contacting the company, or whether a price is given only after a claim review.

Fees, the court filing procedure, minimum claim amounts and languages are shown in the company profiles. They are facts about each company, not criteria.

## Limits

Nothing in this project is legal, tax or investment advice.
